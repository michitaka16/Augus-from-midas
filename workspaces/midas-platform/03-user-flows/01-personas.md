# Midas v1 — User Personas

## Primary: "The Delegator" — Alex

**Profile**: 38, tech executive, $200k in an IBKR account, follows markets via podcasts but doesn't want to log in daily. Has a Wealthfront account for "boring money" but is frustrated by its opacity and lack of regime awareness. Wants to "go big" during clear trends but is terrified of 2020-style overnight gaps without a warning.

**Core need**: "Make me money, but if something scary is happening, ask me first."

**Behavior**:
- Opens the app 2–3x/week (calm markets) or daily (turbulent)
- Wants to understand WHY the system is doing something — not just what
- Will argue with the AI when they disagree ("gold is overbought, I don't care what the model says")
- Expects the system to push back with data, not capitulate
- Values transparency (show me the backtest) over simplicity (just trust us)
- Will pay for value but bristles at percentage-of-AUM fees

**Anti-behaviors** (things Alex will NOT do):
- Read a daily newsletter
- Manually enter 15 trades per week
- Build their own strategy on Composer/QuantConnect
- Accept "we can't explain our model"

**Device usage**: MacBook for deep analysis / debate, iPhone for approvals and quick checks.

## Secondary: "The Validator" — Priya

**Profile**: 45, financial advisor (not RIA), $1.5M across multiple client accounts at IBKR. Uses Midas as a second opinion to validate her own allocation thinking. Subscribes to all 3 model portfolios to compare.

**Core need**: "I want an independent view I can argue with before I place my clients' trades."

**Behavior**:
- Uses debate heavily — tests her own thesis against the AI's
- Compares Midas signals to her own conviction
- Never uses the approval flow (she places trades her own way)
- Values the backtest explorer most — compares her approach's back-tested cost drag against Midas's

**Note**: Priya is a v1 user because the platform is impersonal. She's not getting personalized advice; she's reading a published model portfolio and using the debate agent as a sounding board. This is editorially clean under the publisher exemption.

## Tertiary: "The Learner" — Marco

**Profile**: 26, software engineer, $30k at IBKR, new to multi-asset allocation. Reads r/bogleheads but wants to move beyond the 3-fund portfolio.

**Core need**: "Teach me what regime-aware allocation means by letting me see it work."

**Behavior**:
- Starts with paper trading mode
- Uses debate agent to ask "why are we overweight commodities right now?"
- Explores backtests to build intuition
- Low revenue potential in v1 but high word-of-mouth and community value

## Non-Persona (explicitly NOT our user)

**"The Day Trader" — Kyle**: Wants real-time streaming, options, leverage, 50 trades/day. Midas is not for Kyle. Wrong product, wrong cadence, wrong instrument set.

**"The Set-and-Forget" — Sam**: Wants to deposit and never think about it. Sam wants Wealthfront/Betterment. Midas requires too much engagement (approvals, debate) for Sam.
